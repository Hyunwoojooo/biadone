# Blabee Brand Direction

## Core colors

The product names are the brand source of truth. The following CSS values are screen implementations selected for this site:

- PANTONE® 19-0303 TCX Jet Black → pure black `#000000` for this landing page, per visual approval
- PANTONE® 12-0643 TCX Blazing Yellow → `#FEE715`

`#000000` is the approved visual implementation for the landing page, not a claim that it is Pantone's official RGB conversion.

Pure black is the uninterrupted background for every page-level section. Tonal black surfaces are limited to controls and the product demonstration. Blazing Yellow is reserved for active decisions, primary actions, focus indicators, thin section signals, and the Blabee mark plate; it is not used as a full-width section background.

Warm off-white (`#F3EFE4`) may be used for essential headings and high-priority UI text. Body copy uses warm gray. Pure white is not a default surface or text color.

## Character and interface

Blabee feels warm, attentive, compact, and precise. The existing menu-bar character mark may identify Blabee and signal state, but it must not become decorative filler or overpower the four-choice interface.

## Typography and layout

- System sans for marketing hierarchy and copy
- System monospace for terminal content, labels, shortcuts, and small metadata
- Strong editorial scale, compact controls, generous negative space, and visible alignment rules
- Square corners and borders are preferred over floating cards and diffuse shadows

## Motion

Motion is functional: working, completed, choice surfaced, and same-session review. It runs once and yields to user interaction. Reduced-motion users see the choices immediately.

## Avoid

- Purple or blue AI gradients
- Decorative glow, glass, galaxies, spaceships, or JARVIS metaphors
- Repetitive bento cards or dashboard composition
- Fake product screens, social proof, metrics, or unverified integrations
- Yellow on every surface; the accent is meaningful because it is scarce
