# Blabee Landing Page Anti-patterns

Reject a direction when it weakens the product explanation or makes Blabee interchangeable with a generic AI product.

## Product misframing

- A dashboard, project-management suite, or general chatbot becomes the main metaphor.
- The four-decision interaction is hidden behind decorative mockups.
- Planned behavior is presented as available.
- Trust is implied through invented customer evidence or unverified privacy language.

## Generic visual shortcuts

- Purple-blue AI gradients, glowing orbs, galaxy imagery, or science-fiction control-room motifs
- Repetitive bento grids or floating pills used without information value
- Glass, blur, shadows, and animation applied to every surface
- Oversized vague headlines with too little product explanation
- Meaningless dashboard screenshots or stock integration walls

## Brand imbalance

- The bee character reads as children's entertainment or decorative noise.
- Warm yellow is used so broadly that hierarchy and contrast collapse.
- Developer-oriented precision is replaced by novelty effects.
- Desktop cards merely shrink on mobile instead of recomposing intentionally.

External references may inform composition and rhythm, but never copy another product's logo, copy, trademarked asset, or distinctive artwork.
