The real cost is not the work. It is restarting the context.

Every scattered decision becomes time you have to recover later.

A meeting ends. A decision disappears into chat. A task loses the reason behind it. A document lives in one tool, the deadline in another, and the next step in someone's memory.

BiaDone is built for the moments when you are forced to re-find, re-explain, re-organize, and re-start.

Re-explaining

Re-finding

Re-starting

Returning to a project means rebuilding the mental state from scratch.

Re-deciding

Without context, every next step becomes another judgment call.

What is BiaDone?

A Personal Context OS for carrying work forward.

BiaDone connects the context behind your work — conversations, calendars, documents, decisions, and workflows — into a memory layer that can be recalled, prepared, confirmed, and completed.

It does not wait for you to rebuild the story. It prepares the next step from the context you choose to keep.

Why we are building BiaDone

People should not have to start from zero every time context changes.

Modern work is not short on tools. It is short on continuity.

Every day, people lose time rebuilding the same background, searching for the same decision, and translating scattered information into the next action.

BiaDone exists to make context continuous. We are building a Personal Context OS that remembers what matters with permission, prepares what comes next, and keeps people moving forward without taking control away from them.
